2. Information We Collect (Data Types)

Since the app has a wishlist, a feed, and push notifications, the following data is collected:

  • Account Data: Phone number (required for login via SMS verification), email address (optional for notifications and account recovery), and name (via Google Sign-In or manual entry).
  • Location Data: Pin code provided by the user to track which platform offers the fastest delivery for their location.
  • App Usage & Preferences: Data on products viewed, wishlisted items, search history, and categories browsed (Electronics, Fashion, Beauty, etc.) to curate your personalized feed.
  • Device Identifiers: Device ID (Android ID / iOS Identifier), IP address, device model, and operating system version required to deliver real-time push notifications for price drops.
  • Affiliate Tracking Data: Click data when you click affiliate links to Amazon and Flipkart. These stores may place cookies on your device to track purchases for affiliate commission purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information (Purpose)

We use the information we collect strictly to:

  • Synchronize your shopping wishlist across devices so you can track products for price drops.
  • Deliver real-time price drop alerts via push notifications when products in your wishlist drop in price.
  • Analyse viewing patterns to personalize your feed and show relevant deals based on categories you browse.
  • Monitor app performance and fix technical crashes to improve features and stability.
  • Compare delivery speeds across platforms using your pin code to show which platform offers fastest delivery for your location.
  • Earn affiliate revenue when you buy through our links, which helps us maintain the app.

4. Third-Party Data Processing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share anonymized or technical data with third-party service providers to monitor application stability and user engagement. These third parties process data strictly under their own privacy protocols.

Third-party services we use:

  • Supabase: Database hosting (stores your wishlist, account data, pin code, and app data)
  • Firebase: Analytics (app usage tracking) and push notifications
  • Google Sign-In: Authentication (login via Google, optional)
  • CueLinks Affiliate Network: Universal affiliate tracking and redirect optimization across supported Indian e-commerce stores (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Ajio, Purplle, Tata CLiQ, Snapdeal)
