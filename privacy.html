1. Introduction

Welcome to PriceHop.

PriceHop is a product discovery and price comparison application that helps users compare products, prices, ratings, availability, and delivery information across participating third-party ecommerce platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains how PriceHop collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile application, website, and related services ("PriceHop", "the App", "we", "us", or "our").

By using PriceHop, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Developed & Operated by: Milan Sojan and Josh Jacob Sunil

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact:
Email: support@pricehop.in

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, personalize, and improve PriceHop.

The information we may collect includes the following:

2.1 Account Information

Depending on the sign-in method you choose, we may collect:

  • Phone number used for SMS/OTP authentication
  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account identifiers
  • Authentication information provided through supported sign-in providers

Email may be optional depending on the features you use. We do not collect or store your ecommerce platform passwords or payment credentials.

2.2 Shopping and App Activity

When you use PriceHop, we may collect information about your interactions with the App, including:

  • Products searched for
  • Products viewed
  • Products compared
  • Products added to your wishlist
  • Categories browsed
  • Products interacted with through our Flashcards feature
  • Products you indicate that you like or dislike
  • Preferences and recommendation interactions
  • Deals or offers viewed
  • App features used
  • Links or ecommerce platforms selected through PriceHop

We use this information to provide relevant product comparisons and personalize your experience.

2.3 Flashcards and Personalized Recommendations

PriceHop may use your interactions with our Flashcards feature to understand your shopping preferences.

For example, if you swipe right or otherwise indicate that you like a product, or swipe left to indicate that you do not like a product, this interaction may be used to improve the products and recommendations shown to you.

We may also consider your searches, viewed products, wishlist activity, categories, and other interactions to provide more relevant recommendations.

These recommendation systems are designed to improve your shopping experience and do not determine your eligibility for financial, employment, insurance, healthcare, or other high-impact decisions.

2.4 Location / Delivery Information

PriceHop may ask you to provide a PIN code or similar location information to compare estimated delivery availability or delivery speed across participating ecommerce platforms.

We do not require your precise GPS location for this purpose. If you manually provide a PIN code, we may associate it with your account or shopping activity in order to provide location-specific delivery information and recommendations.

2.5 Device and Technical Information

We may automatically receive certain technical information when you use PriceHop, depending on the permissions and third-party services enabled in the App. This may include:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Device or app identifiers
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Network information
  • General information about how the App is used

This information may be used for security, analytics, troubleshooting, performance monitoring, and improving the App.

2.6 Push Notifications

If you enable push notifications, PriceHop may use a device or application notification identifier to send notifications such as:

  • Price-drop alerts
  • Wishlist updates
  • Important service notifications
  • Product or deal notifications
  • Other notifications you have chosen to receive

You can disable push notifications through your device or App settings, subject to the functionality provided by your device and operating system.

2.7 Information You Provide to Us

We may collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Contact customer support
  • Submit feedback
  • Report an issue
  • Participate in surveys or testing
  • Submit content through features that allow user contributions
  • Communicate with us by email or other supported channels

4. Product Information and Third-Party Ecommerce Platforms

PriceHop is primarily a product discovery and price comparison service.

PriceHop does not directly sell, manufacture, ship, or fulfill the products displayed through the App unless expressly stated otherwise.

Product information displayed through PriceHop may include:

  • Product names
  • Prices
  • Discounts
  • Product images
  • Availability
  • Ratings
  • Reviews or review information
  • Estimated delivery information
  • Seller or platform information
  • Other product-related information

This information may be obtained from participating ecommerce platforms, partners, APIs, feeds, affiliate networks, or other permitted sources.

Prices, availability, ratings, reviews, discounts, and delivery estimates may change at any time. PriceHop does not guarantee that information displayed in the App will always reflect the latest information available on the relevant ecommerce platform.

Users should verify the final price, availability, delivery date, seller, terms, and other details on the relevant ecommerce platform before completing a purchase.

5. Redirects to Third-Party Ecommerce Platforms

When you select a product or shopping option, PriceHop may redirect you to the relevant third-party ecommerce platform.

These platforms may include, among others, Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Tira, AJIO, Snapdeal, Reliance Digital, Tata CLiQ, or other platforms that may be supported by PriceHop.

Once you leave PriceHop and interact with a third-party ecommerce platform, your interaction is governed by that platform's own privacy policy, terms, cookies, tracking technologies, and other policies.

PriceHop does not control how third-party ecommerce platforms collect, use, store, or process information after you leave our App. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of the relevant third-party platform before providing personal or payment information to it.

6. Affiliate Links and Affiliate Tracking

Some links displayed through PriceHop may be affiliate links.

If you click an affiliate link and subsequently make a qualifying purchase on a participating ecommerce platform, PriceHop may receive a commission or other compensation from that platform or affiliate network.

Affiliate tracking may involve cookies, pixels, attribution parameters, or other technologies operated by the relevant third party. For example, participating affiliate programs may include Amazon Associates, Flipkart Affiliate, or other affiliate networks.

PriceHop does not control the cookies or tracking technologies used by third-party ecommerce platforms or affiliate networks after you leave PriceHop. The use of such technologies is governed by the relevant third party's privacy and cookie policies.

7. Ratings, Reviews, and Other Third-Party Content

PriceHop may display product ratings, reviews, product information, or other content originating from third-party ecommerce platforms or other permitted sources.

Such information may be aggregated or presented to help users compare products. PriceHop does not necessarily create or independently verify all third-party ratings, reviews, or product information. The availability, accuracy, completeness, and content of third-party information may change without notice.

8. Our Community Features

PriceHop may introduce community features that allow users to share product reviews, deals, opinions, discussions, recommendations, comments, or other content.

If you use such features, information you voluntarily submit may be visible to other users or the public, depending on the feature. You should not post passwords, financial information, phone numbers, addresses, or other sensitive personal information in publicly accessible content.

Where permitted, PriceHop may moderate, remove, restrict, or review content that violates applicable laws, our terms, or community rules. If your account is deleted, we will handle associated user-generated content in accordance with our deletion practices and applicable legal requirements.
