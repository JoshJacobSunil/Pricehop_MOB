Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

1. Introduction

Welcome to PriceHop ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and related web services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide you with fast, accurate price comparisons and automated price drop alerts:

  • Account & Contact Information: Email address when you join our early access waitlist or create an account.
  • Saved Deals & Alert Preferences: Items you bookmark, target price triggers you set, and notification preferences.
  • Usage & Device Diagnostics: Anonymized app performance metrics, device model, operating system version, and general error logs to ensure smooth functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Deliver instant price drop notifications for items on your watchlist.
  • Personalize deals and discovery feeds according to your preferences.
  • Improve system reliability, latency, and matching precision.
  • Communicate critical product updates and early access invitations.

4. Data Security & Storage

We utilize encrypted transmission protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and secure database storage. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for commercial advertising.

5. Third-Party Retailer Links

When you tap on a deal or comparison link, you will be redirected to the respective retailer's official website or app. Their independent privacy policies and terms govern transactions completed on third-party stores.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. You can also opt out of promotional emails or push notifications directly from your device settings.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our team at privacy@pricehop.in.
